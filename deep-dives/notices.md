# Notices & Announcements — Deep-Dive

## Overview

This guide takes a closer look at the Notices feature in SkoConnect. It's written for school administrators, teachers, and staff who create and manage announcements. By the end, you'll understand notice categories, targeting options, read tracking, and best practices for getting your messages noticed.

---

## Notice Basics

A notice is a message sent from the school to families and students. Notices appear in the **Notices** tab of the mobile app and are delivered as push notifications to recipients' phones.

### Who Can Create Notices

| Role | Can Create | Can Edit/Delete |
|------|-----------|-----------------|
| Admin | ✅ Yes | ✅ Yes (all notices) |
| Teacher | ✅ Yes | ✅ Yes (own notices) |
| Staff | ✅ Yes | ✅ Yes (own notices) |
| Parent | ❌ No | ❌ No |
| Student | ❌ No | ❌ No |

### Notice vs. Event vs. Emergency Broadcast

| When to Use | Feature | Example |
|-------------|---------|---------|
| Sharing information or updates | **Notice** | "Report cards go home Friday" |
| Something with a specific date and time | **Event** | "Parent-Teacher Conference — March 15 at 3 PM" |
| Urgent safety situation | **Emergency Broadcast** | "School lockdown — shelter in place" |

If your message has a date and time attached, create an event instead. If it's a life-safety situation, use an emergency broadcast.

## Creating a Notice

### Step-by-Step

1. Click **Notices** in the left sidebar of the admin portal
2. Click **Create Notice** in the top-right corner
3. Fill in the **Title** — this is your headline (keep it short and specific)
4. Write your message in the **Description** field
5. Choose a **Category** (optional but recommended)
6. Set your **Target Audience** (roles and/or grade levels)
7. Click **Send**

Your notice appears instantly in the mobile app for everyone in your target audience. They'll also receive a push notification on their phone.

> **Important:** Push notifications are delivered when you click **Send**. If you edit the notice later, a new notification is not sent.

## Notice Categories

Categories add a colored label to your notice in the mobile app. This helps families scan their feed and find what's relevant to them.

| Category | Color | When to Use |
|----------|-------|-------------|
| 📢 School Announcement | Green | General school news, policy updates, reminders |
| ⚠️ Alert | Orange | Important time-sensitive updates (not emergencies) |
| 🌟 Holiday | Pink | Holiday schedules, break reminders, school closures |

### Tips for Choosing a Category

- Pick the category that best matches the **primary topic** of your notice
- If a notice covers multiple topics (an event during a holiday), pick the more urgent category (Alert)
- Don't overuse the Alert category — families may start ignoring orange labels if everything is "urgent"

## Targeting Your Audience

### Role-Based Targeting

When creating a notice, you choose which roles receive it:

- **Parents** — reaches all parent accounts linked to students at your school
- **Students** — reaches all student accounts
- **Staff** — reaches teachers and staff members

You can select one, two, or all three roles. Most everyday notices target **Parents** or **Parents + Students**.

### Grade-Level Targeting

You can also limit a notice to specific grade ranges. This is useful for:

- Exam schedules that only apply to certain grades
- Field trip details for a specific class
- Grade-specific announcements (e.g., "Grade 6 immunization forms")

When grade targeting is active, only families with children in those grades see the notice.

> **Tip:** If you're not sure whether to target broadly or narrowly, go broad. It's better for a parent to receive a notice that doesn't apply to them than to miss one that does.

## Read Receipt Tracking

After sending a notice, you can see how many people have opened it.

### How It Works

1. Go to **Notices** in the admin portal
2. Look at the notice in the list — you'll see a read count (e.g., "45 of 120 read")
3. Click on the notice to see more details

The read count updates in real time as people open the notice in the mobile app.

### What Counts as "Read"

A notice is marked as read when the recipient opens it in the app. The count does not include push notifications that were dismissed without opening the app.

### Interpreting Read Rates

| Read Rate | What It Means | Action |
|-----------|--------------|--------|
| 80%+ | Great engagement | No action needed |
| 50-79% | Average | Consider resending or following up |
| Under 50% | Low engagement | Resend with a clearer title, or check if notifications are enabled |

## Editing and Deleting Notices

### Editing a Sent Notice

1. Go to **Notices** in the admin portal
2. Click on the notice you want to edit
3. Make your changes
4. Click **Save**

You can edit the title, description, category, and target audience after sending. However, the push notification was already delivered with the original content.

### Deleting a Notice

1. Go to **Notices** in the admin portal
2. Click on the notice you want to delete
3. Click **Delete**

> **Important:** Deleted notices are removed from everyone's feed. They cannot be recovered. Consider editing instead of deleting when possible.

## Best Practices

### Writing Effective Titles

| Good Title | Why It Works |
|-----------|-------------|
| "Grade 5 Science Fair: Projects Due Friday" | Specific, actionable, has a deadline |
| "No School — Monday, January 15 (National Holiday)" | Clear, date-specific, scannable |
| "Report Cards Going Home Today" | Timely and relevant |

| Weak Title | Why It's Weak |
|-----------|-------------|
| "Important Information" | Vague — doesn't tell the reader what it's about |
| "Please Read" | Every notice should be read — this doesn't help |
| "Update" | Update about what? |

### Notice Frequency

There's no hard limit on notices, but here are guidelines:

- **1-3 notices per week** is a healthy pace
- **More than 5 per week** risks families tuning out
- Group related information into a single notice when possible
- If you find yourself sending daily notices, consider whether some of that information belongs in an event or form instead

### Timing

- Send notices during school hours (8 AM - 4 PM) when families are most likely to check their phones
- Avoid sending notices late at night or on weekends unless it's urgent
- Emergency broadcasts are the exception — send them immediately regardless of time

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | Solution |
|---------|----------|
| Parents say they didn't receive a notice | Check your **Target Audience** — the notice only reaches people in the selected roles and grades. Verify that parents have push notifications enabled on their phones. |
| Notice has a typo after sending | Edit the notice from the Notices page. The in-app content will update, but the push notification already went out with the original text. |
| Low read rate on a notice | Check the title — was it clear and specific? Consider resending with a better title. Also verify the target audience was set correctly. |
| Can't find a notice I created | Check if you accidentally deleted it. Use the search bar on the Notices page. Notices are sorted by date — scroll down for older ones. |
| Notice appears under the wrong category | Edit the notice and change the category. The label updates immediately in the mobile app. |
| Parents see notices from the wrong grade | Check the grade targeting settings. If no grade filter was set, all parents see the notice regardless of grade. |

## FAQ

**Q: Can I schedule a notice to send later?**
A: Currently, notices are sent immediately when you click **Send**. Prepare your notice ahead of time and send it when you're ready.

**Q: Can I attach files or images to a notice?**
A: Notices support text content. For documents or images, consider sharing a link (Google Drive, Dropbox) in the notice description.

**Q: How do I know if a specific parent read my notice?**
A: The read count shows the total number of readers, not individual names. For detailed tracking, ask your administrator about the analytics dashboard.

**Q: What happens if I send a notice to the wrong audience?**
A: Edit the notice and change the target audience. People outside the new audience will no longer see it. However, the push notification already went out to the original group.

**Q: Can students reply to notices?**
A: No. Notices are one-way communications. If a student or parent needs to respond, they should contact the teacher or school office directly.

**Q: How long do notices stay visible in the app?**
A: Notices remain visible until they are deleted by the creator or an administrator. There's no automatic expiration.
