# Student — Complete SOP

## Overview

This guide covers everything you can do as a student in the SkoConnect mobile app. SkoConnect is your school's communication hub — check it for notices, events, forms, and emergency alerts.

As a student, you can:

| Feature | What You Can Do |
|---------|----------------|
| **Home Screen** | See a summary of your school day |
| **Calendar & Events** | View your school calendar |
| **Notices** | Read announcements from your school and teachers |
| **Forms** | Fill out and submit forms your teacher sends you |
| **Emergency Alerts** | Receive and acknowledge urgent broadcasts |
| **Settings** | Manage your profile and notification preferences |

You cannot create notices, events, or forms — those are managed by your school. Your role is to stay informed and respond when your teachers need something from you.

Each section below is self-contained — jump to any section without reading the whole guide.

## Getting the App

### Download

SkoConnect is available for both iPhone and Android:

1. Open the **App Store** (iPhone) or **Google Play** (Android)
2. Search for **SkoConnect**
3. Download and install the app

### System Requirements

- **iPhone**: iOS 14 or later
- **Android**: Version 8.0 or later
- An internet connection (Wi-Fi or cellular data)

## Logging In

### Email and Password

1. Open the SkoConnect app
2. Enter your school email address
3. Enter your password
4. Tap **Log In**

### First-Time Login

If this is your first time, the school may have given you a temporary password:

1. Check your email for a message from SkoConnect
2. Follow the link to create your own password
3. Choose a password with at least 8 characters — mix letters and numbers
4. Log in with your new password

### Forgot Password

1. On the login screen, tap **Forgot Password**
2. Enter your school email address
3. Check your inbox for a reset link
4. Follow the link to set a new password

### Biometric Login

After logging in once, you can enable fingerprint or face login:

1. Go to **More** → **Privacy & Security**
2. Toggle **Biometric Login** on
3. Next time you open the app, use your fingerprint or face to log in

> **Tip:** Biometric login is convenient, but remember your password in case you need to log in on a different device.

## Home Screen

The Home screen is your personal dashboard. It shows a quick overview of what's happening at your school.

### What You'll See

- **Greeting** — a welcome message with your name
- **Summary cards** — counts of unread notices, upcoming events, and forms to fill out
- **Recent activity** — the latest notices and events

### Navigating the App

The bottom of the screen has five tabs:

- **Home** — this dashboard
- **Routine** — your calendar and events
- **Forms** — forms from your teachers
- **Notices** — announcements from the school
- **More** — profile, notifications, privacy, and other settings

Swipe through each tab to see what's there. You can always come back to the Home screen by tapping the **Home** tab.

## Calendar & Events

The **Routine** tab shows your school calendar — events, exams, holidays, and activities.

### Viewing the Calendar

1. Tap the **Routine** tab at the bottom of the screen
2. Events are organized into three sections:
   - **Today** — events happening today
   - **Upcoming** — events scheduled in the future
   - **Past** — events that have already happened
3. Tap any event to see full details:
   - Event title
   - Date and time
   - Location (if provided)
   - Description

### Event Reminders

When your school sets a reminder for an event, you'll get a push notification before it starts. For example, you might get a reminder 1 hour before a parent-teacher conference or 1 day before an exam.

> **Note:** You can only view events on the calendar. Your teachers and administrators create and manage all events.

![Screenshot of mobile app calendar view showing today's events and upcoming schedule](assets/screenshots/mobile-events.jpg)

## Notices

Notices are messages from your school and teachers — exam schedules, event announcements, holiday reminders, and sports updates.

### Browsing Notices

1. Tap the **Notices** tab at the bottom of the screen
2. Scroll through the list of recent notices
3. Each notice shows:
   - **Title** — the subject
   - **Category label** — color-coded tag (Exam, Sports, Holiday, etc.)
   - **Preview** — the first few lines of the message
   - **Timestamp** — when it was sent

### Reading a Notice

1. Tap any notice in the list to open it
2. Read the full message
3. If the notice has an image attached, tap the image to view it full-size
4. Tap **Back** to return to the list

### Notice Categories

Notices are color-coded to help you find what matters:

| Category | Color | What You'll See |
|----------|-------|-----------------|
| School Announcement | Blue | General school news |
| Alert | Red | Important warnings |
| Exam | Blue | Test schedules and results |
| Event | Blue | Event reminders |
| Sports | Green | Sports news and schedules |
| Holiday | Purple | Holiday and closure dates |

> **Tip:** Check the Notices tab at least once a day so you don't miss anything important from your teachers.

![Screenshot of mobile app notices feed showing categorized announcements](assets/screenshots/mobile-notices.jpg)

## Forms

Your teachers may send you digital forms — permission slips, surveys, consent forms, and more. When a form is assigned to you, it shows up in the **Forms** tab.

### Viewing Active Forms

1. Tap the **Forms** tab at the bottom of the screen
2. You'll see two sections:
   - **Active** — forms that need your response
   - **Closed** — forms whose deadline has passed

### Filling Out a Form

1. Under the **Active** section, tap a form to open it
2. Read the title and any instructions at the top
3. Fill in each field:

   | Field Type | How to Answer |
   |------------|---------------|
   | **Text** | Type a short answer (your name, class) |
   | **Email** | Type your email address |
   | **Checkbox** | Tap to check or uncheck |
   | **Dropdown** | Tap to see options, then select one |
   | **Textarea** | Type a longer answer (comments, notes) |

4. Fields marked with a red asterisk (*) are required — you can't submit without filling them in
5. Tap **Submit** when you're done
6. You'll see a confirmation that your form was submitted

### Viewing Submitted Forms

After submitting, find your form under the **Closed** section:

1. Tap the **Forms** tab
2. Scroll to **Closed**
3. Tap a form to see what you submitted

> **Tip:** Don't wait until the last minute. Submit forms as soon as you can so your teacher has time to process them.

## Emergency Alerts

Emergency alerts are urgent messages from your school — safety situations, weather closures, or other critical events. They're marked with a red priority indicator and appear immediately.

### Receiving an Alert

1. You'll get a **push notification** on your phone
2. Open the app — the alert appears at the top of your notices
3. Tap the alert to read the full message

### Acknowledging an Alert

If the school asks you to acknowledge the alert:

1. Read the message carefully
2. Follow any instructions from your school
3. Tap **Acknowledge** to confirm you've read it

Responding helps your school know everyone is safe and informed.

### Priority Levels

| Priority | Color | Meaning |
|----------|-------|---------|
| **High** | Red | Critical — immediate attention needed |
| **Medium** | Orange | Urgent — needs prompt attention |
| **Low** | Yellow | Important — needs awareness |

> **Important:** Keep push notifications turned on for SkoConnect. Go to your phone's **Settings** → find SkoConnect → enable **Notifications**. This ensures you get emergency alerts right away.

## Settings

### Profile Settings

Update your information:

1. Go to **More** → **Profile Settings**
2. You can update your phone number and email address
3. Tap **Save** to confirm

### Notification Settings

Control which notifications you receive:

1. Go to **More** → **Notification Settings**
2. You'll see a **master toggle** to turn all notifications on or off
3. Individual toggles for each type:
   - **Notices** — new announcements
   - **Events** — event reminders
   - **Forms** — new forms from your teachers
   - **Emergency** — urgent broadcasts

> **Important:** Keep **Emergency** notifications turned on at all times. This is how your school reaches you during emergencies.

### Privacy & Security

1. Go to **More** → **Privacy & Security**
2. Enable **Biometric Login** for fingerprint or face recognition
3. This keeps your school information private even if someone else picks up your phone

### Logging Out

1. Go to **More**
2. Tap **Logout**
3. Confirm you want to log out

> **Tip:** Always log out if you're letting someone else use your phone.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | Solution |
|---------|----------|
| Can't log in | Double-check your email address. Tap **Forgot Password** to reset via email. Make sure you're connected to the internet. |
| No notices showing | Pull down on the notices list to refresh. Check your internet connection. Ask your teacher if they've posted any notices. |
| App shows old information | Pull down to refresh. Close and reopen the app. If it's still outdated, check your internet connection. |
| Can't submit a form | Fill in all required fields (marked with *). If it still won't submit, close and reopen the form. Try again. |
| Not getting notifications | Go to your phone's Settings → SkoConnect → enable Notifications. Also check inside the app under More → Notification Settings. |
| Submitted the wrong answers | Contact your teacher right away. They may be able to help you resubmit. |
| Biometric login not working | Make sure it's turned on under More → Privacy & Security. Also check that your phone has fingerprint or face recognition set up. |

## FAQ

**Q: Can I create notices or events?**
A: No — students can only view notices and events. Your teachers and school administrators create and manage all content.

**Q: Can my parents see my forms?**
A: Forms are sent to the person they're addressed to. If a form is sent to you, your parents won't see it unless the school also sends a copy to them. Permission slips and consent forms are usually sent to parents directly.

**Q: How do I change my profile information?**
A: Go to **More** → **Profile Settings**. You can update your phone number and email address there.

**Q: What happens when a form closes?**
A: Closed forms move to the **Closed** section of the Forms tab. You can view your submitted answers but can't make changes. If you missed a deadline, talk to your teacher.

**Q: How do I turn on fingerprint or face login?**
A: Go to **More** → **Privacy & Security** and enable biometric login. You'll also need fingerprint or face recognition set up in your phone's system settings.

**Q: Is the app free?**
A: Yes. SkoConnect is provided by your school at no cost to you. You just need an internet connection.

**Q: Can I use the app on a tablet or computer?**
A: Currently, SkoConnect is designed for phones only. It works on iPhones and Android phones.
