# Student Quick Start Guide

## Overview

This guide walks you through the SkoConnect mobile app as a student — from downloading the app to checking notices and filling out forms your teacher sends you. It takes about 5 minutes. By the end, you'll know how to stay on top of everything happening at your school.

## Prerequisites

- An Android phone (Android 8.0 or later)
- Your **School Code** (provided by your school)
- Your SkoConnect login credentials (provided by your school)
- An internet connection (Wi-Fi or cellular data)

---

## Step 1: Download the App

SkoConnect is available on Android:

1. Open the **Google Play Store** on your phone
2. Search for **SkoConnect**
3. Download and install the app

> **Note:** iOS coming soon. The iPhone app will be available on the App Store once it launches.

The app icon is teal with a white speech bubble and gold signal waves. Once installed, find it on your home screen.

---

## Step 2: Log In

Open the SkoConnect app and log in with the credentials your school gave you:

1. Open the app
2. Enter your **School Code** — this is the code your school gave you that identifies your school
3. Enter your school email address
4. Enter your password
5. Tap **Log In**

If this is your first time, you may need to set a new password. Pick something you'll remember — at least 8 characters with a mix of letters and numbers.

> **Tip:** Your School Code was given to you by your school. If you don't know it, ask your teacher or school office.

> **Tip:** Forgot your password? Tap **Forgot Password** on the login screen. You'll need your School Code and email address to reset it.

![Screenshot of mobile app login screen showing email and password fields](assets/screenshots/mobile-login-screenshot.jpg)

## Step 3: Explore Your Home Screen

After logging in, you'll land on your **Home** screen. This is your personal dashboard — it shows a greeting, a summary of your school day, and quick links to what's important.

The bottom of the screen has five tabs:

- **Home** — your dashboard and overview
- **Routine** — today's schedule and upcoming events
- **Forms** — forms your teacher has sent you
- **Notices** — announcements from your school and teachers
- **More** — profile settings, notifications, and privacy options

Take a moment to swipe through each tab to see what's there.

![Screenshot of mobile app home screen showing student dashboard with summary cards and quick links](assets/screenshots/mobile-home-screenshot.jpg)

---

## Step 4: View Notices

Notices are messages from your school and teachers — things like exam schedules, event announcements, holiday reminders, and sports updates.

1. Tap the **Notices** tab at the bottom of the screen
2. Scroll through the list of recent notices
3. Tap any notice to read the full message

Notices are sorted by date — the newest ones appear first. Some notices have colored labels like "School Announcement" (green), "Alert" (orange), or "Holiday" (pink) to help you spot what's relevant.

> **Tip:** Check the Notices tab at least once a day so you don't miss important announcements from your teachers.

![Screenshot of mobile app notices feed showing categorized school announcements](assets/screenshots/mobile-notices.jpg)

---

## Step 5: Check the Calendar

The **Routine** tab shows your school calendar — events, exams, holidays, and activities:

1. Tap the **Routine** tab at the bottom of the screen
2. You'll see events organized as **Today**, **Upcoming**, and **Past**
3. Tap any event to see details like date, time, location, and description

If your school has set reminders for an event, you'll get a push notification before it starts — for example, a reminder 1 hour before an exam.

> **Note:** You can only view events on the calendar. Teachers and administrators create and manage events.

![Screenshot of mobile app calendar view showing upcoming events and school schedule](assets/screenshots/mobile-events.jpg)

---

## Step 6: Fill Out a Form

Your teachers may send you digital forms — things like permission slips, surveys, or consent forms. When a form is assigned to you, it shows up in the **Forms** tab:

1. Tap the **Forms** tab at the bottom of the screen
2. Look for forms labeled **Active** — these need your response
3. Tap a form to open it
4. Fill in the required fields (marked with a red asterisk)
5. Tap **Submit**

After submitting, you'll see a confirmation. You can check your past submissions under the **Closed** section.

> **Tip:** Don't wait until the last minute. Submit forms as soon as you can so your teacher has time to process them.

![Screenshot of mobile app forms screen showing active forms awaiting student submission](assets/screenshots/mobile-forms.jpg)

---

## Step 7: Respond to an Emergency Alert

In an emergency, your school may send an urgent broadcast. These alerts are marked with a red priority indicator and appear immediately.

If you receive an emergency alert:

1. Open the app — the alert appears at the top of your notices
2. Read the message carefully
3. Follow the instructions from your school
4. Tap **Acknowledge** to confirm you received the alert (if prompted)

Responding to emergency alerts helps your school know that everyone is safe and informed.

> **Important:** Make sure push notifications are turned on for SkoConnect. Go to your phone's **Settings** → find SkoConnect → enable **Notifications**. This ensures you receive emergency alerts right away.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | Solution |
|---------|----------|
| I can't log in | Double-check your **School Code** and email address — use the ones your school gave you. Tap **Forgot Password** to reset it via email. |
| I don't know my School Code | Ask your teacher or school office. The School Code was given to you when your account was created. |
| I don't see any notices | Check that you're connected to the internet. Pull down on the notices list to refresh. If the list is still empty, ask your teacher if they've posted any notices. |
| The app shows old information | Pull down on any screen to refresh. If it still looks outdated, close the app completely and reopen it. |
| I can't submit a form | Make sure you've filled in all required fields (marked with a red asterisk). If the form still won't submit, try closing and reopening it. |
| I'm not getting notifications | Open your phone's **Settings**, find SkoConnect, and make sure **Notifications** are turned on. Also check inside the app under **More** → **Notification Settings**. |
| I accidentally submitted the wrong answers | Contact your teacher right away. They may be able to help you resubmit the form. |

## FAQ

**Q: Can I create notices or events?**
A: No — students can only view notices and events. Creating and managing content is done by teachers, staff, and administrators.

**Q: How do I change my profile picture or update my information?**
A: Go to **More** → **Profile Settings**. From there, you can update your phone number and email address. Profile picture changes may need to be done through your school administrator.

**Q: Can my parents see my forms?**
A: Forms are sent to the person they're addressed to. If a form is sent to you (the student), your parents won't see it unless the school also sends a copy to them. Permission slips and consent forms are typically sent to parents directly.

**Q: What happens when a form closes?**
A: Closed forms move to the **Closed** section of the Forms tab. You can still view your submitted answers, but you can't make changes. If you missed the deadline, talk to your teacher.

**Q: How do I turn on fingerprint or face login?**
A: Go to **More** → **Privacy & Security** and enable biometric login. This lets you log in with your fingerprint or face instead of typing your password every time.

**Q: Is the app free?**
A: Yes. SkoConnect is provided by your school at no cost to you. You just need an internet connection to use it.
