Parent Quick Start Guide

Overview

This guide walks you through the SkoConnect mobile app as a parent — from downloading the app to checking your child’s calendar and filling out forms. The process takes about 5 minutes. By the end, you’ll know how to stay connected with your child’s school.

Prerequisites

  • An Android phone (Android 8.0 or later)
  • Your School Code (provided by your school administrator)
  • Your SkoConnect login credentials (provided by your school administrator)
  • An internet connection (Wi-Fi or cellular data)

Step 1: Download the App

SkoConnect is available on Android:

  1. Open the Google Play Store on your phone
  2. Search for SkoConnect
  3. Download and install the app

Note: iOS coming soon. The iPhone app is in development and will be available on the App Store once Apple Developer registration is complete.

The app icon is teal with a white speech bubble and gold signal waves. Once installed, you’ll find it on your home screen.

Step 2: Log In

Open the SkoConnect app and log in with the credentials your school provided:

  1. Open the app
  2. Enter your School Code — this is the code provided by your school administrator that identifies your child’s school
  3. Enter your email address
  4. Enter your password
  5. Tap Log In

If this is your first time logging in, you may need to set a new password. Choose something you’ll remember — at least 8 characters with a mix of letters and numbers.

Tip: Your School Code was provided by your school administrator. If you don’t know your School Code, contact your school’s main office.

Tip: Can’t remember your password? Tap Forgot Password on the login screen. You’ll need your School Code and email address to reset it.

Step 3: Explore Your Home Screen

After logging in, you’ll see your Home screen. This is your dashboard — it shows a personalized greeting, a summary of your child’s activity, and quick access to important updates.

At the top, you’ll see your name and your child’s name. If you have more than one child at the school, you’ll see a child selector — tap it to switch between your children’s information.

The bottom of the screen has five tabs for navigating the app:

  • Home — your dashboard and summary
  • Routine — today’s events and upcoming calendar
  • Forms — forms that need your attention
  • Notices — announcements from the school
  • More — profile, notifications, privacy settings, and more

Note: Everything you see on the Home screen is specific to your child. If you have multiple children, use the child selector at the top to switch views.

Step 4: Switch Between Children

If you have more than one child at the school, the app lets you switch between their information:

  1. On the Home screen, tap your child’s name at the top of the screen
  2. A dropdown or list appears showing all your linked children
  3. Tap the name of the child you want to view
  4. The Home screen updates to show that child’s notices, events, and forms

You can switch children at any time — the app remembers which child you were viewing last time.

Step 5: View Notices

Notices are announcements from your child’s school — things like event reminders, schedule changes, and important updates.

  1. Tap the Notices tab at the bottom of the screen
  2. Browse through the list of recent notices
  3. Tap any notice to read the full message

Notices are grouped by time — you’ll see the most recent ones first. Some notices have category labels like “Exam,” “Event,” or “Holiday” to help you find what you need quickly.

Tip: The school can target notices to specific grade levels, so you’ll only see notices relevant to your child’s grade.

Step 6: Check the Calendar

The Routine tab shows your child’s schedule — upcoming events, holidays, exam dates, and more:

  1. Tap the Routine tab at the bottom of the screen
  2. You’ll see events organized by time: Today, Upcoming, and Past
  3. Tap any event to see the details — date, time, location, and description

If the school has set reminders for an event, you’ll receive a push notification before it starts (for example, 1 hour before a parent-teacher conference).

Note: You cannot create or edit events from the mobile app — those are managed by the school. You can only view them.

Step 7: Fill Out a Form

Schools use digital forms for things like permission slips, consent forms, and surveys. When a form is sent to you, it appears in the Forms tab:

  1. Tap the Forms tab at the bottom of the screen
  2. You’ll see a list of active forms that need your response
  3. Tap a form to open it
  4. Fill in the required fields (fields marked with a red asterisk are required)
  5. Tap Submit

After submitting, you’ll see a confirmation message. You can view your past form submissions in the Closed section of the Forms tab.

Tip: Complete forms as soon as possible. Some forms have deadlines, and the school may send reminders if you haven’t responded. If you need to make changes after submitting, contact your child’s teacher.

Step 8: Emergency Alerts

In rare cases, the school may send an emergency broadcast — an urgent message about a safety situation. These alerts appear immediately and are marked with a red priority indicator.

If you receive an emergency alert:

  1. Open the app — you’ll see the alert at the top of your notices
  2. Read the message carefully
  3. Follow the instructions provided by the school
  4. Tap Acknowledge to confirm you’ve received the alert (if the school requests acknowledgment)

The school can track who has and hasn’t acknowledged an emergency alert, so it’s important to respond promptly.

Important: Emergency alerts are marked with high priority and are designed to reach you as quickly as possible. Make sure push notifications are enabled for SkoConnect so you don’t miss urgent messages.

Troubleshooting

Problem Solution
I can’t log in Make sure you’re using the correct School Code and the email address the school provided. Tap Forgot Password to reset your password via email.
I don’t know my School Code Contact your school administrator or main office. The School Code was provided when your account was created.
I don’t see my child’s name on the Home screen Your account may not be linked to your child yet. Contact your school administrator to complete the linking.
I’m not receiving push notifications Open your phone’s Settings, find SkoConnect, and make sure Notifications are enabled. Also check the notification settings inside the app (under More → Notification Settings).
The app shows outdated information Pull down on any screen to refresh the content. Make sure you have a stable internet connection.
I submitted a form but want to change my answer Contact your child’s teacher. They can guide you on whether the form can be resubmitted.
I see notices for the wrong grade Check the child selector at the top of the Home screen — you may be viewing a different child’s information.

FAQ

Q: How do I add another child to my account? A: Contact your school administrator. They will link your account to your additional child. Once linked, you can switch between children using the selector at the top of the Home screen.

Q: Can I reply to a notice? A: Currently, notices are one-way communications from the school. If you need to respond, contact your child’s teacher directly using the method your school prefers (email, phone, or in-person).

Q: How do I change my phone number or email? A: Go to More → Profile Settings. From there, you can update your phone number and email address.

Q: Will I see notices from all teachers? A: Yes. You’ll see notices from the school and all teachers at your child’s school. Notices are filtered by grade level, so you only see content relevant to your child.

Q: What happens when a form’s deadline passes? A: Expired forms move to the Closed section in the Forms tab. You can still view your submitted responses, but you can no longer edit or submit the form. Contact your child’s teacher if you missed a deadline.

Q: Is my child’s information private? A: Yes. You can only see information related to your own children. The app includes privacy settings that you can review under More → Privacy & Security. You can also enable biometric login (fingerprint or face recognition) for extra security.
