Teacher & Staff Quick Start Guide

Overview

This guide walks you through SkoConnect as a teacher or staff member — from logging in to sending your first notice and scheduling your first event. The process takes about 5 minutes. By the end, you’ll know how to reach families and keep everyone informed.

Prerequisites

  • A SkoConnect account (created by your school administrator)
  • A web browser (Chrome, Firefox, Safari, or Edge) for the admin portal
  • The SkoConnect mobile app (optional — for viewing on the go)

Step 1: Log In

Your school administrator created your account and sent you login credentials. Here’s how to get started:

  1. Go to your school’s SkoConnect portal
  2. Enter the email address associated with your account
  3. Enter your password
  4. Click Log In

If this is your first time logging in, you may be asked to set a new password. Choose something you’ll remember — at least 8 characters with a mix of letters and numbers.

Tip: If you can’t log in, click Forgot Password on the login page. You’ll receive an email with a link to reset your password.

Step 2: Explore Your Dashboard

After logging in, you’ll land on your Dashboard. This is your home base — it shows a quick overview of your school’s activity, including recent notices, upcoming events, and any forms that need attention.

Take a moment to look around. The sidebar on the left is your main navigation. The sections you’ll use most often are:

  • Notices — send announcements to families
  • Events — add items to the school calendar
  • Forms — create digital forms for parents and students

Note: As a teacher or staff member, you can create and manage notices, events, and forms. You cannot manage users or run enrollment — those tasks are handled by administrators.

Step 3: Create a Notice

Notices are the fastest way to reach families. Here’s how to send one:

  1. Click Notices in the left sidebar
  2. Click Create Notice in the top-right corner
  3. Enter a clear Title — for example, “Grade 5 Science Fair Reminder”
  4. Write your message in the Description field
  5. Under Target Audience, choose who should see the notice — you can target specific roles (parents, students, staff) or grades
  6. Optionally, pick a Category (School Announcement, Alert, Exam, Event, Sports, Holiday) to help families organize their feed
  7. Click Send

The notice appears instantly in the mobile app for everyone in your target audience. They’ll also receive a push notification on their phone.

Tip: Write your notice title like a newspaper headline — short and specific. “Grade 5 Science Fair: Projects Due Friday” is better than “Important Information.”

Step 4: Schedule an Event

Events show up on families’ calendars in the mobile app. Here’s how to add one:

  1. Click Events in the left sidebar
  2. Click Create Event
  3. Enter an Event Title — for example, “Parent-Teacher Conference — Grade 3”
  4. Set the Date, Start Time, and End Time
  5. Add a Location (optional — “Room 201” or “School Auditorium”)
  6. Under Target Audience, select who should see the event
  7. Under Reminders, choose when to notify attendees — 5 minutes before, 1 hour before, or 1 day before
  8. Click Create Event

Families will see this event on their calendar and receive a reminder at the time you selected.

Step 5: Create a Form

Need to collect information from parents or students? Digital forms replace paper handouts and track responses automatically. Here’s how to create one:

  1. Click Forms in the left sidebar
  2. Click Create Form
  3. Enter a Form Title — for example, “Grade 4 Field Trip Permission Slip”
  4. Add your questions using the field types available:
    • Text — short answers (student name, class)
    • Email — email addresses
    • Checkbox — yes/no questions
    • Dropdown — multiple choice from a list
    • Textarea — long answers (comments, concerns)
  5. Click Save

Tip: You can also start from a template. SkoConnect includes pre-built templates for common school forms like field trip permissions, medical consent, and emergency contact forms.

Step 6: View Notices on Your Phone

While the admin portal is where you create content, the mobile app is where you can check what’s happening on the go:

  1. Download the SkoConnect app from the App Store (iPhone) or Google Play (Android)
  2. Open the app and enter your School Code (provided by your school administrator)
  3. Log in with the same email and password you use for the admin portal
  4. Tap the Notices tab to see all recent announcements
  5. Tap the Routine tab to see today’s events and upcoming calendar items

The mobile app is a convenient way to stay informed without needing a computer.

Troubleshooting

Problem Solution
I can’t log in Check that you’re using the correct email address — the one your administrator used to create your account. Try clicking Forgot Password to reset it.
I don’t know my School Code for the mobile app Ask your school administrator for the School Code. You’ll need it to log in to the mobile app.
I don’t see the sidebar navigation Make sure your browser window is wide enough. Try maximizing the window or using a desktop browser instead of a tablet.
My notice didn’t reach everyone Check your Target Audience selection. If you targeted a specific grade, only families in that grade will see the notice.
I accidentally sent a notice with a typo You can edit the notice after sending it. Click on the notice in the list, make your corrections, and save.
I can’t find the Forms page The Forms page appears in the left sidebar. If you don’t see it, you may not have the right permissions — contact your school administrator.
The mobile app shows old notices Pull down on the notices list to refresh. If the problem persists, check your internet connection.

FAQ

Q: Can I edit or delete a notice after sending it? A: Yes. Go to Notices, find the notice in the list, and click on it. You can edit the title, description, or audience. You can also delete it entirely. Note that push notifications may have already been delivered.

Q: Can I target a notice to a specific grade level? A: Yes. When creating a notice, use the Target Audience settings to select specific grades. This is useful for grade-specific announcements like exam schedules or field trip details.

Q: Can other teachers see my forms? A: Yes. All notices, events, and forms are visible to other teachers and staff at your school. This helps everyone stay informed about what’s happening across classrooms.

Q: How do I know if parents have read my notice? A: Notices include read receipt tracking. You can see how many people have opened your notice from the notices list. For detailed read reports, ask your administrator about the analytics dashboard.

Q: What’s the difference between a notice and an event? A: A notice is a message — an announcement, reminder, or alert. An event appears on the calendar with a date and time. If something has a specific date and time attached to it, create an event. If it’s just information to share, create a notice.

Q: Can I schedule a notice to send later? A: Currently, notices are sent immediately when you click Send. For time-sensitive communications, prepare your notice ahead of time and send it when you’re ready.
