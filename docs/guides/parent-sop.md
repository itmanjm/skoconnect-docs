# Parent — Complete SOP

## Overview

This guide covers everything you can do as a parent in the SkoConnect mobile app. SkoConnect is your direct connection to your child's school — you'll find notices, events, forms, and emergency alerts all in one place.

As a parent, you can:

| Feature | What You Can Do |
|---------|----------------|
| **Home Screen** | See a summary of your child's activity |
| **Calendar & Events** | View your child's school calendar |
| **Notices** | Read announcements from the school and teachers |
| **Forms** | Fill out and submit digital forms |
| **Emergency Alerts** | Receive and acknowledge urgent broadcasts |
| **Groups** | View groups your child belongs to |
| **Settings** | Manage your profile, notifications, and privacy |

You cannot create notices, events, or forms — those are managed by the school. Your role is to stay informed and respond when needed.

Each section below is self-contained — jump to any section without reading the whole guide.

## Getting the App

### Download

SkoConnect is available for both iPhone and Android:

1. Open the **App Store** (iPhone) or **Google Play** (Android) on your phone
2. Search for **SkoConnect**
3. Download and install the app

### System Requirements

- **iPhone**: iOS 14 or later
- **Android**: Version 8.0 or later
- An internet connection (Wi-Fi or cellular data)

## Logging In

### Email and Password

1. Open the SkoConnect app
2. Enter your **School Code** — the code provided by your school administrator that identifies your school
3. Enter the email address your school provided
4. Enter your password
5. Tap **Log In**

### First-Time Login

If this is your first time logging in, the school may have sent you a temporary password or a password setup link:

1. Check your email for a message from SkoConnect
2. Follow the link to create your own password
3. Choose a password with at least 8 characters and a mix of letters and numbers
4. Log in to the app with your new password

### Forgot Password

1. On the login screen, tap **Forgot Password**
2. Enter your **School Code**
3. Enter your email address
4. Check your inbox for a password reset link
5. Follow the link to create a new password
6. Log in with your new password

### Biometric Login

After logging in once, you can enable biometric login (fingerprint or face recognition) so you don't need to type your password every time:

1. Go to **More** → **Privacy & Security**
2. Toggle **Biometric Login** on
3. Next time you open the app, use your fingerprint or face to log in

> **Tip:** Your School Code was provided by your school administrator. If you don't know it, contact your school's main office.

> **Tip:** Enable biometric login for convenience, but remember your password in case you need to log in on a different device.

## Home Screen

The Home screen is your personal dashboard. It shows a customized overview of your child's school activity.

### What You'll See

- **Greeting** — a welcome message with your name
- **Child selector** — your child's name at the top (tap to switch if you have multiple children)
- **Summary cards** — quick counts of unread notices, upcoming events, and pending forms
- **Recent activity** — the latest notices and events

### Switching Between Children

If you have more than one child at the school, use the child selector to see information for each child:

1. Tap your child's name at the top of the Home screen
2. A list of all your linked children appears
3. Tap the name of the child you want to view
4. The Home screen, notices, events, and forms all update to show that child's information

> **Tip:** The app remembers which child you were viewing last time. When you open the app, it shows the same child's information automatically.

## Calendar & Events

The **Routine** tab shows your child's school calendar — upcoming events, exam dates, holidays, and activities.

### Viewing the Calendar

1. Tap the **Routine** tab at the bottom of the screen
2. Events are organized into three sections:
   - **Today** — events happening today
   - **Upcoming** — events scheduled in the future
   - **Past** — events that have already happened
3. Tap any event to see full details:
   - Event title
   - Date and time
   - Location (if provided)
   - Description
   - Reminder settings

### Event Reminders

When the school sets reminders for an event, you'll receive a push notification before it starts. Common reminder times include:

- 5 minutes before (for meetings and conferences)
- 1 hour before (for events and assemblies)
- 1 day before (for important dates and deadlines)

> **Note:** You cannot create or edit events — they are managed by the school. You can only view them.

## Notices

Notices are announcements from your child's school and teachers. This is where you'll find important information about schedules, events, exam details, and general updates.

### Browsing Notices

1. Tap the **Notices** tab at the bottom of the screen
2. Notices are sorted by date — the most recent appear first
3. Each notice shows:
   - **Title** — the subject of the notice
   - **Category label** — color-coded (Exam, Sports, Holiday, etc.)
   - **Preview text** — the first few lines of the message
   - **Timestamp** — when the notice was sent

### Reading a Notice

1. Tap on any notice in the list
2. The full notice opens with the complete message
3. If the notice includes an image attachment, you can view it by tapping the image
4. When you're done, tap **Back** to return to the list

### Notice Categories

Notices are color-coded by category to help you find what's relevant:

| Category | Color | What You'll Find |
|----------|-------|------------------|
| School Announcement | Blue | General school news and updates |
| Alert | Red | Important warnings and non-emergency alerts |
| Exam | Blue | Test schedules, study guides, exam results |
| Event | Blue | Event reminders and details |
| Sports | Green | Sports schedules, team announcements |
| Holiday | Purple | Holiday schedules and school closures |

### Grade-Level Targeting

The school can target notices to specific grade levels. This means you'll only see notices relevant to your child's grade. If you have children in different grades, switch between them using the child selector to see each child's notices.

> **Tip:** Check the Notices tab at least once a day to stay up to date on school announcements.

## Forms

Schools use digital forms to collect information from you — permission slips, consent forms, surveys, and more. Forms appear in the **Forms** tab when the school sends them to you.

### Viewing Active Forms

1. Tap the **Forms** tab at the bottom of the screen
2. You'll see two sections:
   - **Active** — forms that need your response
   - **Closed** — forms whose deadline has passed

### Filling Out a Form

1. Under the **Active** section, tap a form to open it
2. Read the form title and any instructions at the top
3. Fill in each field:
   - **Text fields** — type your answer (e.g., student name)
   - **Email fields** — enter a valid email address
   - **Checkbox fields** — tap to check or uncheck (e.g., "I grant permission")
   - **Dropdown fields** — tap to see options, then select one
   - **Textarea fields** — type longer answers (e.g., comments or medical notes)
4. Fields marked with a red asterisk (*) are required
5. When you've filled in all required fields, tap **Submit**
6. You'll see a confirmation message

### Viewing Submitted Forms

After submitting a form, you can find it under the **Closed** section:

1. Tap the **Forms** tab
2. Scroll to the **Closed** section
3. Tap a form to see your submitted answers

### What If I Need to Change My Answer?

Contact your child's teacher or the school office. They can guide you on whether the form can be reopened for changes. Once a form's deadline has passed, it moves to the Closed section and can no longer be edited.

> **Tip:** Complete forms as soon as you receive them. The school may send reminder notices if many parents haven't responded before the deadline.

## Emergency Alerts

Emergency alerts are urgent messages from the school about safety situations — weather emergencies, school closures, security incidents, or other critical situations. These alerts are designed to reach you immediately.

### Receiving an Emergency Alert

When the school sends an emergency broadcast:

1. You'll receive a **push notification** on your phone (make sure notifications are enabled for SkoConnect)
2. The alert appears at the top of your notices feed with a red priority indicator
3. Open the app and tap the alert to read the full message

### Acknowledging an Alert

Some emergency alerts require you to confirm that you've received them:

1. Read the alert message carefully
2. Follow any instructions provided by the school
3. Tap **Acknowledge** to confirm you've received and read the alert

The school can see who has and hasn't acknowledged the alert, so responding promptly helps them account for all families.

### Priority Levels

Emergency alerts come in three priority levels:

| Priority | Color | Meaning |
|----------|-------|---------|
| **High** | Red | Critical situation requiring immediate attention |
| **Medium** | Orange | Urgent situation that needs prompt attention |
| **Low** | Yellow | Important update that requires awareness |

> **Important:** Make sure push notifications are enabled for SkoConnect so you don't miss emergency alerts. Go to your phone's **Settings** → find SkoConnect → enable **Notifications**.

## Groups

Groups are collections of users organized by the school — for example, a "Grade 5 Parents" group or a "School Band" group.

### Viewing Groups

1. Go to **More** (the fifth tab at the bottom)
2. Tap **Groups**
3. You'll see a list of groups your account is associated with
4. Tap a group to see its details and members

You can view groups, but you cannot create or edit them — that's managed by the school.

## Settings

### Profile Settings

Update your personal information:

1. Go to **More** → **Profile Settings**
2. You can update:
   - **Phone number** — add or change your contact number
   - **Email** — update your email address (you'll need to verify the new email)
3. Tap **Save** to confirm changes

### Notification Settings

Control which push notifications you receive:

1. Go to **More** → **Notification Settings**
2. You'll see a master toggle to enable or disable all notifications
3. Below that, individual toggles for each type:
   - **Notices** — new announcements
   - **Events** — event reminders
   - **Forms** — new forms assigned to you
   - **Emergency** — urgent broadcasts (always leave this on)
4. Toggle each type on or off based on your preferences

> **Important:** Keep **Emergency** notifications turned on at all times. This ensures you receive urgent alerts from the school.

### Privacy & Security

1. Go to **More** → **Privacy & Security**
2. You can enable **Biometric Login** (fingerprint or face recognition)
3. This adds an extra layer of security — anyone who picks up your phone won't be able to access your child's school information without your fingerprint or face

### Help & Support

1. Go to **More** → **Help & Support** (or **About**)
2. You'll find answers to common questions and contact information for support

### Logging Out

1. Go to **More**
2. Tap **Logout**
3. Confirm you want to log out

> **Tip:** If you're giving your phone to someone else, log out first to protect your child's information.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | Solution |
|---------|----------|
| Can't log in | Check that you're using the correct **School Code** and the email the school provided. Tap **Forgot Password** to reset via email. Make sure you're connected to the internet. |
| Don't know my School Code | Contact your school administrator or main office. The School Code was provided when your account was created. |
| Don't see my child's name | Your account may not be linked to your child yet. Contact your school administrator. |
| Not receiving push notifications | Check your phone's Settings → SkoConnect → make sure Notifications are enabled. Also check inside the app under More → Notification Settings. |
| App shows outdated information | Pull down on any screen to refresh. Close and reopen the app. Check your internet connection. |
| Can't submit a form | Make sure all required fields (marked with *) are filled in. If the form still won't submit, close and reopen it. Try again. |
| Wrong child's information showing | Tap the child selector at the top of the Home screen and switch to the correct child. |
| Notices from a different school appearing | This shouldn't happen. Contact support if you see notices from a school your child doesn't attend. |
| Biometric login not working | Go to More → Privacy & Security and make sure biometric login is toggled on. Also check that your phone has fingerprint or face recognition set up in your phone's system settings. |

## FAQ

**Q: How do I add another child to my account?**
A: Contact your school administrator. They will link your account to your additional child. Once linked, you can switch between children using the selector at the top of the Home screen.

**Q: Can I reply to a notice?**
A: Notices are one-way communications from the school. If you need to respond, contact your child's teacher using the method your school prefers.

**Q: How do I change my phone number?**
A: Go to **More** → **Profile Settings** and update your phone number. Tap **Save** to confirm.

**Q: Will I see notices for all my children?**
A: When you select a child using the child selector, you'll see notices targeted to that child's grade. Switch children to see notices for your other children.

**Q: What happens when a form deadline passes?**
A: The form moves to the **Closed** section. You can view your submitted answers but can no longer make changes. Contact your child's teacher if you missed a deadline.

**Q: Can I turn off all notifications except emergencies?**
A: Yes. Go to **More** → **Notification Settings**. Turn off Notices, Events, and Forms individually, but keep Emergency turned on.

**Q: Is my child's data private?**
A: Yes. You can only see information related to your own children. The app includes privacy settings you can review under **More** → **Privacy & Security**. You can also enable biometric login for extra security.

**Q: What devices does the app work on?**
A: SkoConnect works on iPhones (iOS 14 or later) and Android phones (version 8.0 or later). It does not currently work on tablets or desktop computers.
