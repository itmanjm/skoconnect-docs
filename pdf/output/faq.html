Frequently Asked Questions

This page answers common questions from all SkoConnect users. Questions are organized by topic — find your category below or search for a keyword.

Getting Started

Q: How do I log in to SkoConnect? A: Open the SkoConnect app (mobile) or go to your school’s portal URL (admin/teachers). Enter the email address your school provided and your password. If this is your first time, check your email for a password setup link.

Q: I forgot my password. How do I reset it? A: Tap Forgot Password on the login screen (mobile app) or click the Forgot Password link on the portal login page. Enter your email address and check your inbox for a reset link. The link expires after a few hours — if it doesn’t work, request a new one.

Q: How do I download the mobile app? A: Open the App Store (iPhone) or Google Play (Android) and search for “SkoConnect.” Download and install the free app. You need iOS 14+ or Android 8.0+.

Q: Can I use SkoConnect on my computer? A: The admin portal (for administrators, teachers, and staff) works in any web browser. Parents and students use the mobile app only — there’s no web version for those roles.

Q: I didn’t receive an invitation email. What should I do? A: Check your spam or junk folder first. Invitation emails sometimes get filtered there. If it’s not there, contact your school administrator. They can verify your email address is correct and request a new invitation be sent. Make sure the email address the school has on file is the one you’re checking.

Admin Questions

Q: How many users can I enroll at once? A: You can upload up to 400 rows in a single CSV file. For larger schools, split your list across multiple uploads. The system automatically skips duplicate emails, so re-uploading is safe.

Q: What format should my enrollment CSV use? A: Your CSV must have these columns in the first row: email, role, first_name, last_name. Optional columns: phone, parent_email (for linking students to parents). See the Admin Complete SOP for a complete example.

Q: How do I add a new administrator? A: Go to Users and click Create User. Set the role to “admin.” The new administrator will receive an invitation email with login instructions. You can have as many administrators as your school needs.

Q: Can I see which parents have read a notice? A: Yes. Notices include read receipt tracking. Open any notice to see how many recipients have opened it. For detailed read reports, check the Analytics page.

Q: How do I reset a user’s password? A: Go to Users, find the user, click on their profile, and click Reset Password. They’ll receive an email with a link to set a new password. You cannot see or set passwords directly.

Q: What’s the difference between a notice and an emergency broadcast? A: Notices are for everyday communication — announcements, reminders, event details. Emergency broadcasts are for urgent situations only — safety incidents, weather closures. Overusing the emergency channel reduces its impact when a real emergency occurs.

Q: Can I edit a notice after sending it? A: Yes. Go to Notices, find the notice, click on it, and click Edit. Note that push notifications may have already been delivered before your edit.

Teacher & Staff Questions

Q: Can I see notices from other teachers? A: Yes. All notices from teachers and staff at your school are visible to you. This helps everyone stay informed about what’s happening across classrooms.

Q: How do I target a notice to a specific class or grade? A: When creating a notice, use the Target Audience settings to select specific roles and grade levels. For class-level targeting, ask your administrator to create a group for your class.

Q: Can I create forms from templates? A: Yes. Go to Form Templates to browse pre-built templates (field trip permission slips, medical consent forms, etc.). Click Use Template to create a copy, customize it, and save.

Q: Can I send a notice from the mobile app? A: Yes. Open the mobile app, go to the Notices tab, and look for the create button. You can send quick notices from your phone. For detailed notices with attachments, use the web portal.

Q: What happens if I delete a notice that parents already read? A: Deleted notices are removed from the feed. Parents who already read the notice will no longer see it. Deleted notices cannot be recovered — consider editing instead of deleting when possible.

Parent Questions

Q: How do I add another child to my account? A: Contact your school administrator. They’ll link your account to your additional child. Once linked, use the child selector at the top of the Home screen to switch between children.

Q: Can I reply to a notice from my child’s teacher? A: Notices are one-way communications. If you need to respond to a notice, contact your child’s teacher directly using the method your school prefers (email, phone, or in-person).

Q: How do I change my phone number or email? A: Go to More → Profile Settings in the mobile app. Update your phone number or email address and tap Save.

Q: Will I see notices for all my children? A: When you select a child using the child selector, you’ll see notices targeted to that child’s grade. Switch children to see notices for each of your children.

Q: What happens when a form deadline passes? A: The form moves to the Closed section. You can view your submitted answers but can no longer make changes. Contact your child’s teacher if you missed a deadline.

Q: Can I turn off notifications except emergencies? A: Yes. Go to More → Notification Settings. Turn off individual notification types (Notices, Events, Forms) while keeping Emergency notifications on.

Student Questions

Q: Can I create notices or events? A: No. Students can view notices, events, and forms. Teachers and administrators create all content.

Q: Can my parents see the forms I submit? A: Forms are sent to the person they’re addressed to. If a form is sent to you, your parents won’t see it unless the school also sends them a copy. Permission slips and consent forms are usually sent to parents directly.

Q: Can I enable fingerprint or face login? A: Go to More → Privacy & Security and toggle Biometric Login on. You’ll also need fingerprint or face recognition set up in your phone’s system settings (under Settings → Face ID/Touch ID on iPhone, or Settings → Security → Fingerprint on Android).

Q: I submitted a form with the wrong answers. Can I change it? A: Contact your teacher right away. They may be able to help you resubmit the form. Once a form closes, you can’t make changes.

Emergency Questions

Q: What is an emergency broadcast? A: An emergency broadcast is an urgent message sent by the school for critical situations — safety incidents, weather closures, or security concerns. These alerts are designed to reach everyone immediately.

Q: How will I know if there’s an emergency? A: You’ll receive a push notification on your phone. Open the SkoConnect app to read the full alert. Make sure push notifications are enabled for SkoConnect in your phone’s settings.

Q: Do I need to acknowledge emergency alerts? A: Some emergency alerts ask you to tap Acknowledge to confirm you’ve received and read the message. This helps the school account for everyone during an emergency. Always acknowledge when asked.

Q: What do the priority colors mean? A: Red (High) means critical, life-safety situations. Orange (Medium) means urgent but not immediately dangerous. Yellow (Low) means important updates that need prompt attention.

Account & Security

Q: Is my data private? A: Yes. Your information is only visible to you and authorized staff at your school. Parents can only see data related to their own children. The app includes privacy settings and optional biometric login for additional security.

Q: How do I enable biometric login (fingerprint or face recognition)? A: Go to More → Privacy & Security in the mobile app. Toggle Biometric Login on. You’ll need fingerprint or face recognition set up in your phone’s system settings as well.

Q: Can someone else access my account if they have my phone? A: Enable biometric login (fingerprint or face recognition) under More → Privacy & Security. This prevents unauthorized access. You can also log out under More → Logout when lending your phone to someone.

Q: What happens to my data if I leave the school? A: Your account will be deactivated. Your data (notices read, forms submitted) is preserved but you won’t be able to log in. Contact your school administrator for details.

General

Q: What devices does SkoConnect support? A: The mobile app works on iPhones (iOS 14+) and Android phones (Android 8.0+). The admin portal works in any modern web browser (Chrome, Firefox, Safari, Edge). There’s no tablet or desktop app version.

Q: Does SkoConnect work offline? A: The mobile app requires an internet connection to load new content. If you’re offline, you can view the last content that was loaded. New notices, events, and forms won’t appear until you reconnect. Pull down to refresh when you’re back online.

Q: How do I contact support? A: Check the Help & Support section in the mobile app (under More), or contact your school administrator for assistance.

Q: Is there a limit on how many notices or events I can create? A: There’s no hard limit. However, sending too many notices can overwhelm families and reduce engagement. Aim for quality over quantity — send notices when you have something important to share.

Q: Can I access SkoConnect from multiple devices? A: Yes. You can log in on multiple devices at the same time — for example, the admin portal on your computer and the mobile app on your phone. Changes made on one device are reflected on the others.

Q: What languages does SkoConnect support? A: SkoConnect currently supports English only. Additional language support may be considered for future updates.

Q: How often is the app updated? A: The app is updated periodically with bug fixes and improvements. Enable automatic updates on your phone (App Store → SkoConnect → Automatic Updates) to always have the latest version.

Q: What should I do if the app crashes or freezes? A: First, try closing the app completely (swipe up from the app switcher) and reopening it. If the problem persists, check the App Store or Google Play for an update. As a last resort, uninstall and reinstall the app — you won’t lose any data since it’s stored on the server.

Q: Can I change the email address associated with my account? A: Go to More → Profile Settings in the mobile app and update your email address. You’ll need to verify the new email before the change takes effect. If you’re an administrator, you can also update email addresses from the Users page in the portal.
