## 4. Staff Training Guide (30-min Session)

> **Usage:** SkoConnect team walks through this during a live Zoom session with school staff. Admin shares this guide afterward for reference.

### Before the Session
- All staff have their login credentials
- All staff have the SkoConnect app installed on their phone
- Test accounts are set up so staff can practice during the session
- Zoom meeting link sent to all participants

### Session Structure

#### Slide 1: Welcome & Why SkoConnect (5 min)
**Talking points:**
- "You're here because **[School Name]** is moving to a better way to communicate with families"
- "Right now, information gets lost in WhatsApp groups, paper notices don't come home, and nobody's sure who's seen what"
- "SkoConnect puts everything in one place — parents download one app and get all school communication there"
- "No more scrolling through 50 WhatsApp messages to find one notice"
- "No more 'I never got the letter'"

**Demo:** Show the mobile app home screen on your phone. Scroll through a few notices.

---

#### Slide 2: Download & Login (5 min)
**Action:** All staff log in during the session.

**Steps:**
1. Open the App Store (iPhone) or Google Play Store (Android)
2. Search "SkoConnect"
3. Download and open the app
4. Enter the email address where you received your invitation
5. Enter the password you set (or click "Forgot Password" if needed)
6. You should see your school name and dashboard

**Troubleshooting:**
- "I didn't get an invitation email" → Check spam, or ask admin to resend
- "I can't find the app" → Search "SkoConnect" (one word, capital S and C)
- "Wrong password" → Use "Forgot Password" on the login screen

---

#### Slide 3: Sending a Notice (5 min)
**Action:** Every staff member sends a test notice.

**Steps:**
1. Tap **"Notices"** at the bottom of the app (or in the left sidebar on web)
2. Tap **"Create Notice"** (or the + button)
3. In the **Title** field, type: "Test Notice — [Your Name]"
4. In the **Description** field, type: "This is a test notice from [Your Name]"
5. Under **Target Audience**, select "Parents" (or your actual target)
6. Tap **"Send"**

**Explain:**
- Notices appear instantly on parents' phones as push notifications
- Parents see notices in their home feed — no searching needed
- You can attach images, add priority levels, and pin important notices
- Urgent notices trigger a second notification to ensure parents see them

---

#### Slide 4: Creating a Form (5 min)
**Action:** One staff member creates a permission slip form live.

**Steps:**
1. Tap **"Forms"** in the app
2. Tap **"Create Form"**
3. **Title:** "Field Trip Permission Slip — Test"
4. **Add fields:**
   - Short text: "Child's full name" (required)
   - Multiple choice: "My child has permission to attend" — Yes / No (required)
   - Long text: "Any allergies or medical conditions?" (optional)
5. Set a deadline (e.g., 3 days from now)
6. Select target audience: "Parents"
7. Tap **"Create"**

**Explain:**
- Parents fill out forms directly in the app — no printing, no returning paper
- You can see exactly who submitted and who hasn't
- Forms can have deadlines, and you can send reminders to non-responders
- Submission data is downloadable as a spreadsheet

---

#### Slide 5: Viewing Submissions & Following Up (5 min)
**Steps:**
1. In the Forms tab, find the form you just created
2. Tap **"View Submissions"**
3. Show the list of responses
4. Explain: "As parents submit, their responses appear here in real time"
5. Point out: "This is where you'll see who hasn't submitted yet — the system tracks this for you"

---

#### Slide 6: Emergency Alerts (3 min)
**Steps:**
1. Show the **"Emergency"** section
2. Explain: "Emergency alerts bypass everything — they go to every user immediately with a distinct notification"
3. "Use this ONLY for real emergencies: school closures, safety incidents, urgent pickup changes"
4. "Emergency alerts appear with a red alert banner and override Do Not Disturb settings"
5. "We hope you never need to use this — but it's here when you do"

---

#### Slide 7: Q&A (2 min)
- Open the floor for questions
- Common questions:
  - "Can I send a notice to just one class?" → Yes, use the target audience filter
  - "Can parents reply to notices?" → Yes, they can reply via the in-app chat
  - "What if a parent doesn't have a smartphone?" → They receive email notifications as backup
  - "Can I schedule a notice to send later?" → Yes, you can set a send date and time

## 8. Troubleshooting & Support

### Common Issues (First-Line Resolution)

| Issue | Solution |
|-------|----------|
| "I didn't get an invitation email" | Check spam/junk folder. If not there, admin can resend from Enrollment page |
| "I can't find the app" | Search "SkoConnect" (one word) in App Store or Google Play |
| "My password doesn't work" | Use "Forgot Password" on login screen |
| "I don't see my child's name" | Contact school admin to verify parent-child link |
| "I'm not getting notifications" | Check phone Settings → SkoConnect → Notifications → Allow |
| "I can't upload the CSV" | Ensure CSV matches the template format exactly. Headers must match |
| "Form submissions aren't showing" | Refresh the page. Check submission_count on the form card |
| "Emergency alert didn't send" | Ensure you have Emergency permission in your role |

### Support Escalation

| Severity | Response Time | Channel |
|----------|--------------|---------|
| 🔴 Critical (platform down) | 1 hour | WhatsApp + Email |
| 🟡 High (can't send notices) | 4 hours | WhatsApp + Email |
| 🔵 Normal (how-to question) | 24 hours | Email |
| ⚪ Low (feature request) | 48 hours | Email |
